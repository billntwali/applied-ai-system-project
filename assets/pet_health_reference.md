# PawPal Health and Behavior Reference

## Medication Timing Matrix
Use a simple morning and evening medication matrix with checkbox tracking for each pet. Record exact administration times, appetite status, and any side effects to prevent missed or duplicated doses.

## Post-Surgery Recovery Checklist
After surgery, monitor incision condition, cone compliance, appetite, hydration, and stool quality twice daily. Keep activity low and contact a veterinarian if swelling, discharge, or lethargy increases.

## Anxiety Decompression Protocol
For anxious dogs or cats, start with a predictable decompression protocol: quiet zone, low-stimulus walks or play, short training loops, and reward calm behavior. Avoid sudden schedule changes.

## Mobility Support for Senior Pets
Senior pets with stiffness benefit from traction mats, low-impact movement, short warm-up walks, and elevated bowls. Spread activities into shorter blocks instead of one long session.

## Weight Management Signals
Track weekly weight, treat count, and body condition notes. If weight rises for multiple weeks, tighten treat portions and increase low-impact enrichment before escalating intensity.

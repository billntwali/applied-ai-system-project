# PawPal Knowledge Base

## Daily Routine Foundations
Pets do best with consistent routines. Keep feeding, exercise, litter cleanup, and medication at similar times each day. Short, repeatable routines are easier to maintain than long one-off plans.

## Medication Safety
Double-check medication names, doses, and timing before each administration. Never give another pet's medication. If a dose is missed, consult your vet for the safest next step instead of doubling doses.

## Dog Exercise and Enrichment
Most dogs need daily physical activity plus mental stimulation. Split walks into manageable sessions and add enrichment tasks such as scent games, puzzle feeders, and short training repetitions.

## Cat Comfort and Stress Reduction
Cats prefer predictable feeding windows, clean litter boxes, and quiet recovery spaces. Place litter boxes away from food and water areas. For multi-cat homes, use multiple stations to reduce resource guarding.

## Senior Pet Care
Senior pets usually benefit from shorter, more frequent care blocks. Watch for mobility changes, appetite changes, and hydration shifts. Keep high-priority health tasks early in the day when energy is highest.

## Hydration and Heat Awareness
Fresh water should always be available. During warm weather, schedule walks and vigorous activity during cooler hours and monitor for panting, lethargy, or overheating.

## Multi-Pet Household Coordination
When caring for multiple pets, separate high-priority tasks (feeding, medication) from optional tasks (extra play or grooming). This helps ensure critical care always fits within limited time windows.

## Emergency Escalation
If a pet is unresponsive, has trouble breathing, has severe bleeding, or may have ingested toxins, treat it as an emergency and contact an emergency veterinarian immediately.
